## Conventions

- Each page is one bullet under its parent section.
- Status marker at end of line: `(planned)` or `(live YYYY-MM-DD)`.
- One-line purpose, imperative voice, audience-first ("convince
  evaluators that …"), not "show pricing tiers").
- Internal redirects, canonicals, and cross-property links live
  inline as sub-bullets.
